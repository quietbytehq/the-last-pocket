# The Last Pocket — audio production pack

Use the exact filenames below. Export stereo MP3 at 44.1 or 48 kHz, high quality (256–320 kbps). Put the files into the player's `audio/` folder and restart. Keep the original exports and applicable redistribution licence records. Optional missing files are silent; the original four impact placeholders remain until replaced.

Gameplay music: calm upscale hotel-lobby lounge music; understated, elegant and relaxing at every tier, with subtle changes in instrumentation rather than growing louder or frantic. Music and effects have separate mute switches (N / M and Pause).

Music: instrumental, seamless loop, no fade-in or final cadence, no vocals, 60–120 seconds. Keep it quieter than impacts. Effects: one isolated event, immediate attack, no leading silence, no clipping, no music. Voices: one clean dry spoken line, friendly comic confidence, natural delivery, no background music or sound effects. Centre the voice in the stereo field.
